Elementals are the rawest expression of the same material [[Asgorath]] drew from to make the dragons — fire-pressure, stone-memory, the deep cold of unmeasured time — except that elementals were never remembered into a fixed, named form the way dragons were. They are creation's material still partially in its unworked state: aware, animate, but never given the deliberate, individuated shape Asgorath reserved for her greatest creation.

This is why elementals, almost universally, lack the personality, memory, and ambition that define dragons despite sharing an origin point with them. A fire elemental is not a lesser dragon. It is closer to a fragment of the same primordial fire that became dragons elsewhere, left to exist in its rawest state because [[Asgorath]]'s attention moved on before she finished naming everything she touched.

[[The Elemental Planes]]
Where elemental material exists in overwhelming concentration rather than as scattered fragments within Nyr itself, it forms the elemental planes — vast regions of nearly undiluted fire, water, earth, or air, native habitats for elementals in numbers and intensities the material world could not sustain. These planes are not Asgorath's deliberate construction in the way Nyr itself was; they are closer to the overflow of her First Age labor, the excess material that never made it into the finished world at all.
