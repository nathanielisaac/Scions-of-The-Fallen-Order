Humans were the first humanoid shape to stabilize, appearing in scattered, disconnected pockets across early [[Nyr]] with no single point of origin — a detail that has frustrated generations of [[Velkros's Archivists]], who would very much like a tidier story to file. Humans are widely theorized to be [[Primus]]'s proportions taken to their most basic, least specialized expression: short-lived, adaptable, capable of building quickly because they have so little time in which to do it.
