Elves carry a more specific origin: early elven communities show consistent, ancient signs of [[Sehanine Moonbow]]'s attention from ages before her domains fully solidified. The traditional elven account holds that the first elves were dreamers who never fully woke — mortal enough to live and die, but permanently a half-step closer to the [[dream-plane]] than other humanoids, which accounts for the long elven lifespan, the cultural reverence for the moon, and the discomfort many elves feel in spaces with no natural light at all. [[Astral elves]], a distinct lineage including spacefaring families who settled the Astral Plane generations ago, represent communities that moved even closer to that boundary deliberately.
