Humanoids are not any single god's deliberate creation. They are what happened in the space [[Primus]]'s laws left open — bipedal, tool-using, capable of language and structure, because [[Primus]]'s earliest laws of physics and proportion permitted exactly that shape to be viable and nothing in the First Age's making forbade it. No god sat down and decided humanoids should exist. They emerged the way a valley emerges between two mountains: as the necessary consequence of everything around them.

[[Halflings]], [[Gnomes]], [[Orcs]], and the Rest
The remaining major humanoid lineages — halflings, gnomes, orcs, [[half-elves]], [[half-orcs]], and others — are generally treated by Velkros's Archivists as secondary humanoid expressions: viable shapes within Primus's tolerances that stabilized later, regionally, without a clean single-god account. Orc cultures in particular maintain old oral traditions suggesting an ancestral closeness to Panzuriel's earliest chaos before the world was fully ordered, though most orc communities today have no more inherent connection to chaos than any other people, and the church of Panzuriel — to the extent it can be called a church at all — has never claimed them.
