Dragons are [[Asgorath]]'s deliberate, named creation — not made from nothing, but remembered into existence, pulled from elemental memory she found already half-sleeping in the world's mountains, trenches, and volcanic roots. The Genesis of Nyr is explicit on this point: she did not invent dragons. She recognized them in the raw stone and gave them names, and naming, for a goddess of primordial elemental force, is functionally identical to creation.

The Chromatic and Metallic Divide
The split between [[chromatic dragons]] (associated with destructive elemental forces and, in the oldest dragons, outright cruelty) and [[metallic dragons]] (associated with protection, wisdom, and the eventual emergence of [[Bahamut]]) is not, according to the oldest draconic oral histories, an original feature of Asgorath's design. It emerged generations after the first dragons walked [[Nyr]], as different elemental lineages responded differently to contact with the young world's other forces — chromatic lineages drifting toward the volcanic, destructive aspect of Asgorath's breath, metallic lineages drifting toward its patient, mountain-deep stillness.

Some of the oldest and darkest chromatic bloodlines carry a more troubling history: deliberate corruption by Panzuriel's influence in the deep ages, twisting Asgorath's original work toward cruelty that she did not intend and has never forgiven. The existence of shadow dragons and other deliberately corrupted draconic lines remains, per the Pantheon of Nyr, an open wound between Asgorath and Panzuriel that has never closed.

[[Primordial Dragon Blood]]
The purest expression of Asgorath's original breath survives, in diluted form, in certain ancient bloodlines — true Primordial Dragon Blood, distinct even from ordinary dragon blood, carrying a harmonic resonance with the world's first making that nothing else in [[Nyr]] fully replicates. This blood is exceptionally rare. Most chromatic and metallic dragons today carry only a fraction of the original resonance; true Primordial purity is now found in only the oldest and most isolated lineages, if it can be found at all.
