[[Dwarves]] are, by their own oldest oral traditions, [[Moradin]]'s clearest mortal expression — not literally made by him, but shaped across early generations by such close, sustained proximity to his forge-domain that the influence became hereditary. The first dwarves mined deep into stone because something in the rock called to something newly present in them, and [[Moradin]], recognizing kinship rather than creating it outright, simply claimed them as his own. Dwarven culture's emphasis on craft, family, and oaths kept across generations is, by this account, less a value system adopted than a divine resonance inherited.
