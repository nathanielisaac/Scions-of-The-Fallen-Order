Plant creatures are [[Melora]]'s slowest and most patient creation, predating even the beasts that came to live among them. Where Asgorath's breath produced the dragons in a single deliberate act and beasts as quick overflow, [[Melora]]'s wild growth unfolded gradually, across a much longer span of the First Age, with no single moment of completion — plant life in Nyr was never finished being made, in the same sense that the wilderness itself is never finished growing.

Sentient or mobile plant creatures — the rarer, stranger end of the category — tend to emerge in places where [[Melora]]'s wild attention has lingered with unusual intensity: old-growth forests untouched by mortal hands for centuries, swamps where the natural world has had no competing influence to answer to, places where the wild has simply had enough time and enough freedom to become something closer to deliberate.
