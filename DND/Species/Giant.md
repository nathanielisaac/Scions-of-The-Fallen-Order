Giants are among the oldest humanoid-adjacent shapes in [[Nyr]], predating the stabilization of human, elven, and dwarven proportions by a significant span of the First Age. The oldest draconic and dwarven oral histories agree, unusually, on a single point: giants represent an earlier, larger-scale draft of Primus's structural template for mortal life — viable, functional, but eventually superseded by smaller, faster-breeding, more adaptable proportions as the world filled in around them.

Giants did not disappear when the smaller humanoid shapes stabilized. They simply became rarer, retreating to remote mountains, frozen wastes, and other regions where the older, larger-scale draft of Primus's design remained practical and where smaller competition was slow to arrive. Giant societies often carry a deep, half-mythologized memory of having come first — a claim that smaller humanoid scholars tend to find irritating precisely because the evidence, frustratingly, supports it.
