Dragonborn occupy an unusual position: they are humanoid in shape but carry, in diluted form, the same elemental memory [[Asgorath]] breathed into the first dragons. Most dragonborn lineages emerged generations after the dragons themselves, in regions where draconic and humanoid populations lived in close, sustained contact — the precise mechanism is debated even within draconic scholarship, but the clearest physical evidence is consistent: dragonborn blood carries faint elemental resonance, stronger in some bloodlines than others, and the strength of that resonance appears to correlate with proximity to a Primordial source.


DM NOTE  Dragonborn bloodline strength is directly relevant to Ignael. His own resonance is unusually strong for a dragonborn of unremarkable lineage, which his Asgorath boon explains in play — but the deeper reason (his connection to the Dragonmaw Clan's Primordial blood) should remain a slow reveal rather than something this document spoils outright to the table.
