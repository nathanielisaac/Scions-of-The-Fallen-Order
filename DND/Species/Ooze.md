Oozes occupy the strangest theological position of any creature type in [[Nyr]], because their existence is, in the strictest reading of [[Primus]]'s own First Law, a kind of standing exception. Primus's laws specify structure — form, boundary, consistent shape. An ooze has none of these in any fixed sense, and yet it persists, moves, and consumes, in apparent violation of the very axiom [[Primus]] spoke into being at the dawn of everything.

The most widely accepted theological explanation, advanced cautiously by Primus's own Calibrators, is that oozes represent matter that was assigned a law at some point but lost or never received the specific shape-component of that law — material that is bound by physical principles in every other respect but was never told, so to speak, what its boundary should permanently be. This makes oozes, in Calibrator terms, the closest thing in Nyr to a genuine bug in the underlying structure of reality, and Primus's clergy find them quietly, deeply unsettling for exactly that reason.
