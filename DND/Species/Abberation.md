Aberrations are the one creature type that the Genesis of Nyr's compilers have never been able to satisfyingly place, and the Vel'Kara Remembrance Society's official position, after considerable internal disagreement, is simply to say so plainly: aberrations did not arise from Primus, Panzuriel, Asgorath, Melora, Myrkul, Sehanine, or any other deity active in Nyr's own cosmology. They predate record. They do not fit the categories the rest of this document relies on.

The leading theory, advanced by a minority of Velkros's senior Archivists and treated with considerable unease by everyone else, is that aberrations entered Nyr from somewhere genuinely outside the disagreement between Primus and Panzuriel that produced everything else — that they are not a product of Nyr's creation myth at all, but visitors, or residue, from some other reality's argument entirely, one that has nothing to do with the world the party currently walks through.

DM NOTE  This is intentionally left vague and slightly ominous. Aberrations are the one category in this document built to NOT resolve cleanly into the existing cosmology — useful precisely because it means you can introduce a deeply strange, off-cosmology threat later without it needing to connect to Primus, Panzuriel, or the Device at all. Treat this as a sealed compartment you can open whenever you want something to feel genuinely foreign to Nyr's own mythology.
