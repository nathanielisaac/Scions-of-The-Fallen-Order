The rank your party's wizard would need to access the refinement process.

Only a few dozen exist at any given time.

Requirements:

- Pass the Trial of Ascension.
- Demonstrate exceptional magical ability.
- Demonstrate wisdom, restraint, and character.

Privileges:

- Access to [[Arcane Refinement]].
- Entry into the Celestial Vaults.
- Participation in Conclave deliberations.
- Authority to sponsor future candidates.

Symbol:

- A crystal star suspended within a golden ring.
