## First Impressions

After days of scorching desert travel, the party sees a patch of green on the horizon.

Towering date palms sway around a massive crystal-blue spring.

White sandstone buildings rise from the dunes.

Colorful tents surround the permanent settlement, creating a constantly shifting outer city.

The smell of spices, tea, and exotic foods fills the air.

Hundreds of travelers from distant lands gather here.

Unlike most cities, Miraj never truly sleeps.

Caravans arrive at all hours.

# Population

As a major stopping point the settlement is widely diverse

Humans - the largest population, many generations have lived here

Halflings - Famous merchants and caravan leaders

Genasi - Viewed as blessed by the oasis

Tieflings - traders, scholars, and negotiators

Many many travelers


# Core values
Hospitatlity - refusing water to a thirsty traveler is one of the greatest sins imaginable. even enemies are often offered water before negotiations

Trade - Information is as valauble as gold. new from disntand lands spread rapidly. 

Reputiation - Deals are often sealed by one's word. A dishonest merchant can quikly find themselves unwelcome
