A title granted, not earned.

The Conclave bestows it upon individuals who fundamentally change magical understanding.

A wizard may live centuries and never see one.

Examples:

- Creator of teleportation circles.
- Founder of an entirely new school of magic.
- Savior of the island during magical catastrophe.

Privileges:

- Exempt from most laws.
- Permanent seat among the Conclave advisors.
- Their writings become required study.

Symbol:

- Seven crystal stars orbiting a central gem.
