A mythical rank.

The last Ascendant lived over a thousand years ago.

Many believe the title is merely legend.

An Ascendant is said to understand magic so completely that they no longer cast spells—they simply will reality to change.

Stories claim Ascendants:

- Walked among the gods.
- Created floating cities.
- Bound stars to the heavens.
- Helped weave the magical foundations of the world.

No living member of Aetheris officially holds this rank.
