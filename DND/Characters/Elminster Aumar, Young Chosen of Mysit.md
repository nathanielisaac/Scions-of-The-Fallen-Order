#ImportantNPC 
Age: 31

Wizard (CR 20)

One of the youngest High Loremasters ever appointed.

Already considered one of the greatest living mages.

Unlike later in life, he still enjoys field work.

He refuses to stay behind a desk.

He has personally recovered dozens of cursed relics.

He immediately recognizes the significance of the Muryland Device...

...but has no intention of taking it by force.

His philosophy:

> "If I steal it from them, they'll never trust me when they truly need me."

Instead he watches.

Quietly.

Patiently.
