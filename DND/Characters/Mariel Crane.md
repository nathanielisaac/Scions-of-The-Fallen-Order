# Mariel Crane  —  
### Gear overseeing the northern territories, including Wafeld
Mariel has never met the party directly but has read Zandwyn's reports on them with real interest — adventurers who destroyed Fort Blackreach and survived Lady Ruth represent exactly the kind of unpredictable, capable asset the Clock likes to have a relationship with before someone else does. She is measured, businesslike, and entirely uninterested in the party's politics so long as their contracts are profitable and their reputation doesn't become a liability.
