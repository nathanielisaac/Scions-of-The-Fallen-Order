#PlayerCharacter 

Son of [[Scanlan Shorthalt]]
My name is Gil Shorthalt. I am an elf that came from a [[Thistlewood|secluded poor village]]. My mother and I are farmers. Making coin off of our crops. Taking care of acres of land with only two people is hard work. Thankfully our village folk are kind enough to help us out. My father Scanlan Shorthalt is my father. He never was apart of my life. I’ve only heard stories of him through my mother as I was still an infant, only remembering him as a blur. I am ashamed of my connection to him as I’ve asked about him through the village folk. Learning that he has slept with multiple people of any race and of any gender. This is what made me change my name to Gil Viveron taking my mothers last name. As a way to spite his. 

  

Every few months when the crops are ripe and ready for harvest my mother and I would take them to the nearby town to sell them. The town would always be filled with people bustling about. A lavish market place with various products from places a yonder. But what always caught my attention was the town square. It had people playing music and dancing about. Smiling with joy and seemed like they lost all of their worries. I knew I wanted this in my life as my resentment for my father is what’s been holding my happiness down. But getting an instrument is expensive and all the money we get from our crops is for food on the table and the cloths on our bodies. I knew I won’t be able to get money the normal way.

  

This is where I took a turn in my life and decided to join the thieves guild. I learned how to steal and live in the shadows. Making money not only for me but for my family. After all the missions I’ve gotten from the guild I earned their trust and became one of the few taking missions directly from the Babayaga himself Kug B. Stroganoff the head master of the thieves guild. Being in the guild has been helpful and all but I know this life isn’t for me. So I decided to leave when I’ve had enough money to keep me a float in this society and get my instrument. 

  

I practiced day and night learning from musicians on how to master my trombone. After years of practice and the growing of my siblings I decided to become a traveling bard. Leaving my family behind to travel for the joy of others and spreading my name one village and town at a time. After many travels I came across [[The Kingdom of Aethelgard.canvas]] and met a [[Sir Kaelen|companion]]. Now him and I travel about.
