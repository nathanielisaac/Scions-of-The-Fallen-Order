#ImportantNPC
[[Star Sage]] (retired)

Relationships: 
**[[Lord Garrick Thornvale]]**

A respected lord who governed several villages within Falhurst.

Garrick served as a military commander during his younger years and expected his son to follow a similar path.

**[[Lady Elowen Thornvale]]**

A scholar and amateur historian.

She encouraged Quinlan's intellectual curiosity and secretly funded many of his early studies before he attended Aetheris.

**[[Sir Edmund Thornvale]]** (Deceased)

The heir to the family estate.

A celebrated knight who defended Falhurst during border conflicts.

His death left a lasting mark on Quinlan and eventually forced him to take a greater role in local leadership despite preferring academic life.

**[[Seraphine Valeheart]]** (Deceased)

A renowned professor of ancient civilizations at Aetheris.

The two met as young researchers and spent years exploring ruins throughout Nyr.

Many of Quinlan's discoveries were made alongside her.

Her death from illness twenty years ago changed him profoundly.

### Daughter

**[[Lyra Thornvale]]**

A diplomat and advisor to several noble houses.

She inherited her father's intelligence and political instincts, though not his magical gifts.

### Grandson

**[[Alden Thornvale]]**

A promising student currently studying at Aetheris.

Many faculty members quietly compare him to a young Quinlan.

# 18-29

Quinlan earned a scholarship to Aetheris after demonstrating extraordinary magical talent.

While many students pursued elemental or destructive magic, Quinlan focused on:

- Warding
- Divine studies
- Planar theory
- Magical containment
- Ancient civilizations

During his studies he uncovered references to forgotten cults, sleeping powers, and relics that most scholars dismissed as myth.

He became obsessed with a recurring symbol:

**A chain wrapped around a broken crown.**

The symbol belonged to a secretive organization few believed still existed.

The Order of the Chains.

# 30-38

Upon completing his studies at Aetheris, Quinlan could have remained among the faculty. Several professors even recommended him for positions within the Academy's Arcane Council. 

Instead, he accepted an invitation from the Order of the Chains.
