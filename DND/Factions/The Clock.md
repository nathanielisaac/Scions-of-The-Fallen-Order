#group

The Clock is not a nation, a noble house, or a single guild hall — it is a standing, world-spanning marketplace for professional violence, information, and recovery, organized loosely around regional cells called Faces, each overseen by a senior member known, with characteristic bluntness, as a Hand. The thieves guild the party has already dealt with through Zandwyn operates as one Face among many — Wafeld's Face, specifically — answering, however loosely, to a structure considerably larger than Zandwyn himself ever let on.
