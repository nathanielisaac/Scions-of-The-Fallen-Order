#group 
Relationship with [[The Party]]:
	None

The Harpers organize themselves into Circles. Every major region has its own Circle, and each Circle answers to High Council representatives elsewhere in Nyr. They oppose Primus. They oppose Panzuriel. They want Lord Saxton stopped. They would destroy cults that threaten innocent life. In nearly every practical goal, the Harpers and the Order of the Chains want the same outcome.
